**05/27/2026**  
It has been a while since I visited this secret garden.

A few days ago, I slept and dreamt about D.S. graduating, and he came to me to say goodbye. Still that smile, still that silliness, still that tight hug, still that squeeze, still that good wishes for each other, still that butterfly in the stomach, still that good laughs. He did not kiss me in my forehead, though. Even though it took a while for us to calm down and got recovered from the breakup, I am glad that now we are able to look directly into each other eyes and hug each other like a good friend and still have respect for each other and wholeheartedly look out for each other and want the best for each other. The last time I saw him in person was the beginning week of Spring semester 2026. It was hard to believe two years had passed since the last time we talked to each other back in 2024. Time flies. Almost anytime, we were both startled when seeing each other haha. Like, we can still recognize each other from afar and from across the room. Our eyes still looking for the familiar figure, our ears still soothe themselves with familiar voice.   

I am very glad that the friendship land between me and him did not erose or turn hard. Luckily, it stays there, and it still has shadow for me whenever I visit it--a memory land for me to sometimes go down and rest and take break away from my current reality. He does remind me to move forward, to keep going, and to have hope, and to continue love, and to continue exploring what I love, espcially writing and literature. Love myself, love others, and love all the journeys ahead. 

Love you, D.S.  

Goodbye, and thank you for being a good part and a good man in my life.
